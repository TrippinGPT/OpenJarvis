# PaperLab Overview

## Purpose

PaperLab is the disciplined paper-trading and market-learning lane inside the Trippin ecosystem.

It exists to help users:

- study market behavior
- practice simulated decision-making
- build review discipline
- log and refine hypotheses
- learn without real-money exposure

PaperLab is not a trading-execution product.

## Core safety boundary

This lane is paper only.

- fake money only
- no wallets
- no private keys
- no swaps
- no live order routing
- no real brokerage execution
- no real-money promises
- no guaranteed outcomes

If a concept crosses that boundary, it is out of scope for PaperLab.

## What the paper lane is for

PaperLab is for:

- education
- workflow discipline
- simulated scenario practice
- review and journaling
- signal interpretation practice

It solves a different problem than a trading app.

It is not trying to place trades.

It is trying to teach a repeatable loop:

- observe
- label
- simulate
- log
- review
- learn
- refine

## Who it is for

PaperLab is for:

- beginners who need structure without financial risk
- intermediate users who want cleaner review habits
- builders who want a safe market-learning lane under the Trippin umbrella
- future community users who benefit from disciplined simulation rather than hype

## What it is not

PaperLab is not:

- a live trading platform
- a wallet product
- a swap UI
- a broker connection layer
- a real-money scalper
- an execution bot
- a profit guarantee engine

It is a simulation and learning lane.

## Workflow shape

PaperLab should feel like a clean learning loop.

### 1. Observe

Look at the setup, context, and signal without forcing a trade idea too early.

### 2. Label

Name the scenario:

- momentum
- breakdown
- range
- mean reversion
- low-confidence noise

### 3. Simulate

Define the paper action with fake money only:

- entry idea
- invalidation
- target logic
- scenario conditions

### 4. Log

Capture:

- why the simulated action was considered
- what the signal was
- what the scenario conditions were
- what would have invalidated the idea

### 5. Review

Check what actually happened versus what was expected.

### 6. Learn

Extract:

- what held up
- what was noise
- what was misread
- what should change next time

### 7. Refine

Turn the review into a cleaner scenario rule, checklist, or future simulation filter.

- **Dex PaperScalper** — the advanced fake-money DEX-style drill lane for later scenario work.

## First user/test loop

A good first PaperLab loop should be simple:

1. choose one scenario
2. label the setup
3. define one paper action
4. log the reasons
5. review the outcome later
6. capture one lesson

That is enough to validate the lane.

No wallet. No real order. No fake “trading genius” theater.

## Suggested outputs

Useful outputs for the first active PaperForge phase:

- lesson logs
- session recap reports

These outputs fit the lane better than execution tooling right now.
