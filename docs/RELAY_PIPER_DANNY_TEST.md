# Relay Piper Danny Test

## Purpose

Test `en_US_danny_low` as a possible better Piper candidate for the Relay Companion / Smartmouth Relay voice.

## Why Danny

The sample direction sounds closer to a polished human command-center assistant feel than Lessac, Amy, or LJSpeech.

## Current status

`en_US_danny_low` is a candidate only. It is not final.

## Safety rules

- Local WAV tests only.
- No autoplay.
- No app integration.
- No microphone/audio capture.
- No voice cloning.
- No real-person or copyrighted-character impersonation.

## Test criteria

- Clarity
- Speed
- Robotic quality
- Tone
- Command-center feel
- Smartmouth delivery
- Final fit decision
